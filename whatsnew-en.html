1.0.6

A new logo, English, new section addresses and a big fix for saving

New logo The site has a new logo: a rating scale from red to green with a needle, like a gauge. It now appears in the browser tab on every page, on your phone's home screen and on review pages. The old letter is gone: the new mark reads the same in both languages.

English The ☰ menu now has a language switch: 🌐 English. All pages, windows, hints, picture captions and this changelog switch to English. Your choice is remembered, and the same button takes you back. Text written by people (nicknames, notes, comments) stays as it is.

New addresses Sections now open at /opinion/ and /wheel/. Old links keep working and take you to the right place, so there's nothing to change. On the site the sections are still called Opinion and Wheel.

Editing a rating no longer loses data If you open a rating for editing and reload the page, saving it no longer wipes the status, pros, cons, year and favorite star.

Favorites and the journal A star set in your profile no longer flips back. The ratings journal no longer disappears after a reload, and a rating deleted on another device also disappears from your profile on this one.

Two tabs If Opinion is open in two tabs, they no longer overwrite each other: what you save in one appears in the other right away.

Drafts and search An unfinished comment and the search on the All ratings page no longer reset while you use your profile.

Clearer sign-in If your sign-in has ended, a bar with a “Sign in” button appears at the top: the ratings on your device are safe, and before the site just kept quiet about it. If you can't sign in, you now see the real reason instead of a general password error.

Changing your email A new email starts working only after you enter the code sent to that address, and the old address gets a notice. This goes live together with email codes.

Small things Ratings without a score no longer show “0.0” in exports. Windows close with Esc even if you clicked outside a field first. On phones the ☰ button no longer jumps to the second row of the header. Your profile button, when you're already on your profile, and the logo on the home page no longer reload the page with an animation, they just take you back to the top. Photo uploads now honestly tell you how long to wait if you add too many in a row. There's more protection against password guessing and data tampering.

29 October 2026version 1.0.5

1.0.5

Profile editing, achievements with emblems, titles of your choice and quick rating edits

Profile editing The button with squares next to “Rate something” no longer opens a window, it turns on editing mode right in your profile. Blocks became widgets on a 12-column grid, like a phone's home screen. Hold the ✥ cross in a block's corner and drag it anywhere: to any column, higher or lower. The block lands exactly where you drop it and its neighbors move aside smoothly: first sideways into free space, and if there's none, downward. Drag the handle on the right edge to change the width (one column per step) and the handle on the bottom edge to change the height (40 pixels per step). If the content doesn't fit the set height, the block gets a scrollbar, and a double click on the bottom handle sets the height back to fit the content. The eye icon hides a block: in editing mode it looks pale and says “Hidden”, and friends don't see it at all. Showcases became separate blocks too: the “＋ Showcase” button at the bottom adds a new one, and each showcase has a pencil and a trash can. Changes don't apply on their own: there are “Save” and “Cancel” buttons at the bottom, and the panel tells you when there are unsaved changes. “Cancel” puts the profile back as it was, including deleted showcases. On phones the blocks form a list, and dragging changes their order. You can also move blocks and change their width with the arrow keys. Friends see your profile the way you saved it.

Titles of your choice You now choose the titles under your nickname yourself. In editing mode each title has a cross, and “＋ Title” opens a list of all your unlocked titles: for the number of ratings, the average score, a favorite topic, masterpieces found and every achievement earned. Below that you can see which titles are still locked and what they take. You can show up to four. The order is up to you too: drag a title with the mouse or a finger (or use the arrow keys). Until you pick any, titles are chosen automatically, as before.

Achievements “Badges” have been renamed “Achievements”. Instead of emoji, each one has its own drawn emblem on a colored plate: time in the club is green, the number of ratings is blue, and each special achievement has its own color. Earned ones have a check mark and a glow, the rest have a progress bar with percentages. At the top is an overall bar showing how many achievements you've unlocked.

Quick edits in your profile The card of your own rating in your profile now has an “Edit” button. Set scores by tapping the cells, and edit pros, cons and the note right there, without going to Opinion. The total is recalculated at once. Next to it are small icons: ★ favorite, 🔗 review link and “Picture or text” (opens the right window in Opinion).

A tidier rating menu An expanded rating in Opinion now has two prominent buttons: a light “Edit” and a red “Delete”. Favorite, compare, review link and “Picture or text” became small icons on the right, each with a tooltip. Favorited ratings have a yellow star.

Planned and In progress Ratings now have the statuses “◷ Planned” and “▶ In progress”. With these you can save a rating with no score at all: for example, a film you're going to watch or a game you're playing now. You can add scores later. “Filters” in the ratings has a “Status” section where you can show only what's planned or in progress. Films and series now say “✓ Watched” instead of “Completed”, and old film ratings were renamed automatically. In your profile, in the All ratings block and on the All ratings page there's a new “All · ◷ Planned · ▶ In progress” switch with a count on each: one tap shows what's planned and what's in progress.

Music fills itself in Music covers are now searched not only on Apple Music but also on Deezer, which has many small artists missing from iTunes. You can search by artist alone, without a title. Pick a cover and the artist, track or album title, “Single” or “Album”, year and genre fill in by themselves. The artist's name is suggested too: as you type, real names from Deezer and Apple Music appear under the field, and Deezer even understands typos (“deathmerie” → deathmarried). If you choose an artist's photo, their exact name is filled in with it. The camera icon on the artist's circle is gone: tap the circle itself to set a photo. Fixed: an artist's avatar sometimes said “set” when the browser had no room to save it. Now the site tries to save a smaller picture, and if that doesn't fit either, it tells you so honestly.

The “Administrator” title Site admins now have a red “Administrator” title in their profile. It always comes first.

Browser storage Rating photos and covers took up too much space in the browser, so it ran out quickly and even artist avatars weren't saved. Now pictures are stored separately, there's plenty of room, and old photos move there on their own as soon as you open Opinion. And if the browser still refuses to save a rating, the site tells you instead of keeping quiet.

Your own numbers in your profile The number tiles under your profile header can now be customized. In editing mode each tile has a cross, tiles can be dragged, and “＋ add a number” opens a catalog that shows each number's value right away. It has the basics (ratings, average and best score, masterpieces, ratings of 10 and below 5, favorites, with photos and with notes, from Letterboxd, genres, achievements, days in the club, ratings this week and this month, longest streak), statuses, in total and separately for films and series, anime, games and books (watched, beaten, read, in progress, planned, dropped), plus the number of ratings and the average score for each of your categories. You can show up to 12 numbers. Tiles stretch to the full width: however many there are, the row has no gaps.

Small things The first item in the ☰ menu is now “All ratings” in a colored frame. Genres in the Opinion ratings moved inside “Filters”: the separate “🎭 Genres” button is gone, and the selected genre shows on the “Filters” button itself. The pencil next to your nickname in your profile is gone: you change your nickname by tapping it anyway. Comments under your own rating in Opinion are separated from the buttons by a line and some spacing.

An honest “updated” A friend could show “ratings updated just now” even though they rated something at night and had simply opened the site. Now the time comes from the rating itself: a rating made at 3 a.m. shows 3 a.m., and just visiting the site doesn't change the time.

Announcements A bar with an announcement may sometimes appear at the top of the site: news, a maintenance break or just something festive. It appears by itself, without a page reload, and closes with a cross, after which it won't come back until there's a new one.

More motion Covers and avatars pop slightly when they finish loading, scores bounce when they appear on screen, and icons in tabs and the ☰ menu wobble when pressed.

Small fixes If the browser runs out of room for a rating's photo, the photo no longer disappears after a reload.

Email code Accounts are protected by a code from an email: when you sign up, 6 digits are sent to your email, and without them the account won't be created, so nobody can sign up with someone else's email. When you sign in from a new phone or computer, a code also comes after the password, and then that device is remembered for 30 days. The email may land in “Spam”. Codes will start arriving a little later, as soon as everything is ready.

Comments, avatar history, a music remote in the wheel, review links and new themes

Review link Your ratings in Opinion now have a “🔗 Share a link” button. Music in the wheel The music block has been redone: two cards, “🎶 Background” and “🏆 Win”, each with one big “Turn on” or “Listen” button and one list with tracks, your own file and “No music” all together. A volume slider on top changes the volume of the music and wheel sounds at once, even while everything is playing. Tracks no longer start with silence: VI KA, Chopin, Serious Sam and others skip the empty seconds at the start, and the win song loads while the wheel spins and plays without a pause. The wheel checks your own files too and skips silence at the start. Mortal Kombat plays again: the author barred the old video from being shown on other sites, and the track now comes from a copy that can be embedded. If you pick your own uploaded track from the list, the wheel no longer stumbles and says that it's playing.

Themes: 8 light and 8 dark Light themes are now always light: Mint, Lavender, Sky and Peach used to darken in the evening when a phone or computer switched to night mode, and Sand and Sakura got dark shadows. Only “Auto” darkens with the time of day, and it says so. New themes: light Lemon and dark Amethyst, Coffee, Vampire and Graphite (pure black, for OLED screens). The menu shows each theme as a small card in its own colors, and the cards slide in one by one. Text on colored buttons in dark themes is now readable.

Hints for newcomers They're now on every page: on the home page what's where, in Opinion how to rate, in the Wheel how to spin, in your profile what's there. A hint is a bright card with a waving hand. It doesn't hide by itself, even once you've rated something or spun the wheel, only with the “Got it 👍” button. You can always open it again: with the “❓ Help” button in the header, the round “❓” in the bottom right corner (it appears when the header scrolls away) or the “?” key. On phones the hint switches to the right tab by itself and scrolls to itself. Tabs on phones now have icons.

Twelve reactions You can now respond to a friend's rating with twelve emoji instead of three: 🔥 fire, 😂 lol, 😍 love it, 🐐 legend, 👍 agree, 🗿 based, 🤔 debatable, 😭 to tears, 💀 brutal, 🤡 clownery, 👎 miss and 🙅 disagree. The caption shows on hover and above the emoji row, and the emoji you pick bursts out in a fan.

More stickers, no duplicates The wheel and profile got 46 more Papich stickers and 41 Twitch emotes (catJAM, Aware, Clueless, Bedge, GIGACHAD, HUH, peepoShy and others). Duplicates are gone: 13 identical pictures with different names were removed from the list, and anyone using one had it replaced with the remaining copy automatically.

More motion Buttons across the site spring when pressed, cards below the screen slide up when you scroll to them, numbers in statistics count up, and notifications slide in from the top instead of fading in.

A more reliable profile Changing your email, nickname, username and password in your profile now goes through completely or not at all: before, with a wrong password, your email or nickname could still change. Titles, notes and comments are cleaned of invisible characters that are used to fake how a name or link looks. Double-clicking “Save” on a nickname no longer sends it twice.

Photos from links no longer disappear A picture from a link is now downloaded right away and stored like a regular uploaded photo: you can crop it, and it's on all your devices. Before, the rating kept only someone else's link, and links copied on a phone are often temporary or forbid display on other sites, so the photo vanished after a while. If a link leads to a page rather than the picture itself, the site says so. Without an account, a link is added the old way, with a warning. Cropping a saved photo and photos in tier list pictures work again too.

Showcases and profile blocks Your profile now has a “Showcases and blocks” button. A showcase is your top 3, top 5 or top 10 from any topic: for example, “Top 5 games” or “Favorite films”. It holds the top by score or the ones you pick yourself, in the order you want, shown as posters or a list. There you can also hide any profile block: numbers, titles, “Favorites”, all ratings, badges, friends, the histogram and categories. The layout is stored in your account, so friends see it too.

The “All ratings” page The “Open all” button in your profile opens a separate page with all your ratings. You can view them as a list, a grid, an S–D tier list or in detail, with scores for each criterion, notes, pros and cons. You can pick one or more topics, search and sort. The page works for friends' profiles too.

Covers for games, films, books and anime As with music, Opinion now suggests covers while you type a title: games from Steam, films and series from IMDb, books from Open Library, anime and manga from AniList. A tap sets the cover as the rating's photo and adds the year. If AniList doesn't respond, anime covers come from Kitsu or MyAnimeList. These topics need you to be signed in.

The friends window on any page The “Friends” button in the header now opens a window right where you are instead of sending you to Opinion. Friends in the window now have avatars in a colored frame with an “online” dot. To add a friend, there's a separate “Add a friend” button that expands a row for their @username.

Ratings open in a window in your profile Tapping any rating in your profile, in showcases and on the All ratings page no longer sends you to Opinion, it opens a card right there: photo, topic, score, all criteria with descriptions and scales, notes, pros and cons, and the date. A button at the bottom of the card opens the rating in Opinion. In Opinion the second “Friends” button is gone from the friends row in “Mine”: the friends window opens with the header button, and the row only shows “Requests” when there are any.

Friends' ratings instead of “Mine” The “Mine” button in the Opinion ratings became “Friends”: it's a shared feed of all your friends' ratings, each showing whose it is, without your own ones. Above the feed you can choose one friend. The second “Friends” button on the right is now “All ratings”, which opens the page with all your ratings.

Subscribing to friends' ratings A friend's profile now has a “Subscribe to ratings” button. Sign-up without surprises The browser no longer fills your email into the “Username” field. If something has been typed in the sign-in or sign-up form, clicking outside doesn't close the window, and your nickname, username, email and sign-in login are remembered even if you close it. The password isn't stored: the browser itself remembers it.

Profile always top right On every page, the home page, Opinion, the Wheel and your profile, the top right has a button with your avatar and nickname that opens your profile. Without an account it says “Sign in”. “Customize profile” in your profile now opens the settings right away instead of a window with one more button.

Search for friends In “Add a friend” you don't have to type the whole @username: as you type the start of a @username or any part of a nickname, matching people with avatars appear below. A tap sends a request, or accepts it if that person already sent you one. Change your banner right away While you choose the visible part of a newly set banner, the “Banner” button is no longer locked: a tap saves the position and opens the menu, so you can pick another banner right away. Choosing the visible part now works without an account too.

Number of changes per version “What's new” now shows under each version number how many changes it has.

Comments At the very bottom of your profile there's now a comment wall, like on Steam: friends write to you, you write to them. Avatar and banner history Tap your avatar to open a menu: “Upload new” and your past avatars. The “Banner” menu now has your past banners. Import from Letterboxd You can bring your film ratings over from Letterboxd. There, open Settings → Import & Export → Export Your Data, and on the site open the ☰ menu → “⚙️ Settings” → “🎬 Import from Letterboxd” on any page (or open casualhub.pages.dev/opinion/#letterboxd) and upload the whole downloaded archive or ratings.csv. Friends can see link covers Fixed: covers set by a link (Letterboxd posters, pictures from a direct link) were visible only to the rating's owner and didn't reach friends.
